Privacy Policy

Application: OL x 3GALLC — Last updated: February 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy describes how OL x 3GALLC ("the Application"), developed by 3GA LLC, handles data when connecting to QuickBooks Online via the Intuit API.

2. Data We Access

The Application accesses the following data from your QuickBooks Online account:

  • Invoice records (creation and retrieval)
  • Customer information (name, licence number)
  • Product/service items (SKU, pricing)
  • Company information

3. How We Use Your Data

Data accessed from QuickBooks Online is used exclusively to:

  • Automate invoice creation from supplier delivery records
  • Generate weekly sales reports required by the BC Liquor Distribution Branch (DSWR)
  • Reconcile sales data between suppliers and QuickBooks Online

4. Data Storage

The Application runs locally on the authorized user's computer. QuickBooks data is processed in memory and stored temporarily as local JSON files for report generation. No data is transmitted to external servers, cloud storage, or third-party services.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or distribute any QuickBooks Online data to third parties. Generated DSWR reports are submitted solely to the BC Liquor Distribution Branch as required by regulation.

6. Authentication

The Application uses OAuth 2.0 to authenticate with QuickBooks Online. Access tokens are stored locally in an encrypted credentials file on the user's machine. We do not store Intuit usernames or passwords.

7. Data Retention

Temporary data files are stored locally for the duration needed to generate reports. Users may delete local data at any time. Disconnecting the Application from QuickBooks Online revokes all access immediately.

8. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or to request data deletion, contact: admin@osoyooslarose.com
