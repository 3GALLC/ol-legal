1. Overview

OL x 3GALLC ("the Application") is an internal business tool developed by 3GA LLC for Osoyoos Larose Estate Winery Ltd. The Application connects to QuickBooks Online via the Intuit API to automate invoice management and generate regulatory sales reports (DSWR) for the BC Liquor Distribution Branch.

2. License Grant

This Application is licensed exclusively to authorized employees and representatives of Osoyoos Larose Estate Winery Ltd. for internal business use only. The Application is not distributed to the general public.

3. Permitted Use

The Application may be used to:

  • Create and query invoices in QuickBooks Online
  • Retrieve customer and product information from QuickBooks Online
  • Generate BCLDB-compliant DSWR CSV reports

4. Data Handling

The Application accesses QuickBooks Online data solely for the purposes described above. No QuickBooks data is shared with third parties. OAuth credentials are stored locally on the authorized user's machine and are never transmitted to external servers.
